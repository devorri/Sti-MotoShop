INTRODUCTION

Project Context

Motor shops play an important role in supporting motorcycle owners by providing spare parts and repair services. To operate efficiently and remain profitable, these businesses rely on effective management of their daily operations (Saputra & Fauzi, 2023). Among the most critical aspects of operations are inventory, sales, and customer management. According to Sudirga et al. (2021), inventory management refers to the process of monitoring product availability, stock levels, and the movement of items. Effective inventory management ensures that products are neither overstocked nor out of stock. Sales management, on the other hand, involves recording and tracking customer transactions, including product purchases, quantities, and total sales. Proper sales management ensures accurate financial records and smooth transaction processing (Ascon et al., 2023). Meanwhile, customer management focuses on handling customer information and interactions, which is essential for building relationships and encouraging repeat purchases (Aulia, 2022).

However, Conde et al. (2025) stated that many small-scale motor shops still rely on manual methods such as notebooks or basic spreadsheets to manage these operations. These methods are time-consuming and highly prone to human error. In terms of inventory management, manual recording may lead to inaccurate stock counts, making it difficult to determine product availability (Agboola et al., 2022). Similarly, a recent study indicates that manual sales management often leads to errors in recording transactions, which can result in incorrect sales data and financial inconsistencies (Yuniarto & Siregar, 2025). Furthermore, the absence of a structured customer management system makes it difficult to identify repeat customers or track their purchase history (Najwa et al., 2023). As a result, businesses are unable to implement effective strategies to retain customers or improve service quality. These issues can lead to delays in transactions, poor decision-making, and reduced customer satisfaction, ultimately affecting the overall profitability of the business.

In response to these challenges, many businesses are adopting computerized systems to improve operational efficiency and data accuracy (Șișu et al., 2024). A web-based system, in particular, provides a centralized platform where inventory, sales, and customer data can be stored, processed, and accessed in real time. By automating these processes, businesses can minimize errors, improve transaction speed, and enhance overall management capabilities (Divina et al., 2025).

The proposed project, titled Web-Based Inventory and Sales System with Membership Management for Boss Rap Motor Shop, aims to address these operational issues. The system will integrate three main components: inventory management, sales management, and membership management. The inventory management module will monitor stock levels and automatically update product quantities after each transaction. The sales management module will record and process customer purchases accurately and efficiently. The membership management module will store customer information, track purchase history, and identify repeat customers through a structured system. Additionally, the system will incorporate barcode scanning functionality to improve the speed and accuracy of product identification during inventory handling and sales transactions. It also supports quick customer identification for walk-in members.

Specifically, the membership component addresses the lack of a formal customer management approach in the shop. Currently, customer information is not systematically recorded, making it difficult to recognize loyal customers or analyze purchasing behavior. By introducing a membership system, the shop will be able to register customers, monitor their transaction history, and analyze purchasing behavior. This enables the implementation of reward mechanisms, identification of frequent customers, and development of strategies to increase customer retention.

Overall, the proposed system seeks to replace the existing manual processes of Boss Rap Motor Shop with an integrated web-based solution. By improving inventory accuracy, streamlining sales transactions, and establishing a structured membership system, the project aims to enhance inventory accuracy, accelerate transaction processing, and enable data-driven decision-making, ultimately enhancing customer satisfaction and business performance.

Purpose and Description of the Project

The primary purpose of this project is to design and develop a Web-Based Inventory, Sales, and Membership Management System for Boss Rap Motor Shop. The system aims to modernize and streamline the shop’s daily operations by replacing its existing manual and semi-digital processes with a centralized, integrated, and computerized platform. Through this transformation, the project seeks to improve operational efficiency, enhance data accuracy, and support better decision-making for the business.
Based on the initial interview conducted with the shop owner, Boss Rap Motor Shop currently operates using a combination of manual recording and informal digital methods. Inventory and sales transactions are primarily tracked through handwritten records and basic monitoring practices. In addition, the shop actively accepts customer orders through its Facebook page, where customers inquire about product availability and place orders online. Once a purchase is confirmed, customers are given two options: they may either pick up the items at the store or request delivery, wherein the shop books a third-party courier service such as Lalamove, with the delivery cost shouldered by the customer. While this approach allows the business to reach a wider customer base, it lacks a structured system for recording transactions, tracking inventory changes, and managing customer data efficiently.
These existing practices present several operational challenges. Manual inventory tracking increases the risk of inaccurate stock counts, which may lead to overstocking or stock shortages. Similarly, sales transactions conducted through Facebook messaging are not automatically recorded in a centralized database, making it difficult to maintain consistent and reliable sales records. The absence of an integrated system also results in delays in retrieving information, potential duplication of data, and limited visibility of overall business performance. Furthermore, customer information from online transactions is not systematically stored, preventing the shop from identifying repeat customers or analyzing purchasing behavior.
In response to these challenges, the proposed system will provide a comprehensive and structured solution by integrating inventory management, sales processing, and membership management into a single web-based platform. The system will enable real-time monitoring of product stock levels, ensuring that inventory data is consistently updated after every transaction. By automating inventory updates, the system reduces the likelihood of human error and provides accurate information on product availability at any given time.
For sales management, the system will support both walk-in and online transactions. It will allow staff to process in-store purchases efficiently while also recording orders that originate from the system. Each transaction will be automatically stored in the system, ensuring that all sales data is consolidated in one database. This improves the accuracy of financial records and enables faster transaction processing. Additionally, the integration of barcode scanning functionality will further enhance operational efficiency by allowing quick product identification during sales and inventory handling, reducing reliance on manual input
The system will also incorporate a membership management feature to address the lack of structured customer data handling. Through this feature, customers can be registered within the system, and their information, including contact details and transaction history, will be securely stored. This allows the business to identify repeat customers, monitor purchasing patterns, and implement strategies aimed at improving customer retention. Membership records may also support features such as customer identification during transactions and the potential implementation of rewards or promotional programs, thereby enhancing customer engagement and loyalty.
Another important component of the system is its reporting capability. The proposed platform will generate comprehensive and organized reports on sales performance, inventory status, and product movement. These reports will provide valuable insights that can assist the shop owner in evaluating business performance, identifying fast-moving or slow-moving products, and making informed decisions regarding stock replenishment and sales strategies. By having access to accurate and timely reports, the business can improve its planning and overall management.
Moreover, the web-based nature of the system ensures accessibility and flexibility. Authorized users can access the system through an internet connection, allowing the shop owner to monitor operations even outside the physical store. This is particularly beneficial given that a portion of the shop’s transactions already occurs online through Facebook, as it creates a more seamless integration between online inquiries and recorded sales data.
Overall, the proposed Web-Based Inventory, Sales, and Membership Management System aims to replace the fragmented and manual processes currently used by Boss Rap Motor Shop with a unified and efficient solution. By integrating key business functions into a single platform, the system is expected to improve data accuracy, reduce operational delays, enhance customer management, and support data-driven decision-making. Ultimately, the project seeks to contribute to the growth and sustainability of the business by providing a reliable technological foundation for its operations.

Objective of the Study

General Objective
The main objective of this study is to design and develop a web-based inventory, sales, and membership management system that enhances the efficiency, accuracy, and reliability of product, transaction, and customer data management for Boss Rap Motor Shop. The system aims to address the limitations of the shop’s current manual and informal processes by providing an integrated platform that supports both in-store and online operations.

Specific Objectives

To achieve the general objective, the study aims to accomplish the following:

To develop a comprehensive inventory management module that ensures accurate and real-time monitoring of product stocks.
This module will enable the efficient management of product records by allowing authorized users to add, update, and organize product information. It supports accurate monitoring of stock levels and ensures that inventory data is consistently maintained. The system aims to improve inventory control, reduce errors, and enhance the overall efficiency of stock management within the business.

To develop an integrated sales management module that processes and records transactions efficiently and accurately.
This module will facilitate the efficient processing and recording of sales transactions. It ensures that transaction details are properly captured and organized, supporting accurate sales monitoring and record-keeping. The system aims to improve transaction efficiency, reduce errors, and provide reliable sales data for business operations.


To develop a membership management module with QR code-based identification to support structured customer data handling and customer retention.
This module will enable the registration and secure storage of customer information, including names, contact details, and transaction history. It will support the identification of repeat and high-value customers and facilitate the analysis of purchasing behavior. The inclusion of QR code-based identification will enhance customer recognition and improve transaction efficiency, contributing to better customer engagement and retention.

To develop a reporting module that provides accurate and comprehensive business performance insights.
This module will generate reports on sales performance, inventory status, and customer activity. It will assist the shop owner in monitoring product movement, identifying fast-moving and slow-moving items, analyzing sales trends, and supporting data-driven decision-making for business improvement.

To develop an integrated system that connects the sales, inventory, and reporting modules to ensure accurate and synchronized financial and transaction data within the system.
This feature will record payments made through digital channels such as e-wallets and online banking. It ensures that all online payment transactions are properly documented and linked to corresponding sales records within the system, supporting accurate financial tracking, transparency, and easier reconciliation of accounts.

Scope and Limitation of the Study

Scope
This study focuses on the development of a Web-Based Inventory, Sales, and Membership Management System for Boss Rap Motor Shop. The system will cover the integration of inventory management, sales processing, membership management, reporting, and digital payment recording into a centralized platform to improve operational efficiency and data accuracy.

User Authentication and Access Control Module
This module allows authorized users such as the shop owner and employees to register and securely access the system through a login process. It includes role-based access control to ensure that only permitted users can perform specific system functions.
Inventory Management Module
This module allows the management of product records, including adding, updating, deleting, and categorizing products. It supports stock-in and stock-out processes and automatically updates inventory levels after each transaction. The system also integrates barcode scanning for product identification, enabling faster processing of inventory updates and sales transactions.
Sales Management Module
This module handles both walk-in and online transactions, including orders received through Facebook inquiries. It records product purchases, quantities, prices, and transaction details. The system automatically computes total sales, generates transaction records, and updates inventory in real time. Barcode scanning is used during walk-in transactions to improve speed and accuracy in product selection and processing.
Membership Management and Loyalty Module
This module allows the registration and secure storage of customer information, including names, contact details, and transaction history. It supports customer tracking and the identification of repeat customers. The system generates QR code-based membership cards that are used for customer identification during transactions. The module also supports basic membership benefits such as loyalty tracking and customer engagement features to encourage repeat purchases.
Reporting Module
This module generates comprehensive reports on sales performance, inventory status, product movement, and customer activity. It provides insights such as fast-moving and slow-moving items and supports data-driven decision-making for business operations.
Online Payment Recording Module
This module records and tracks digital payments made through e-wallets and online banking platforms. It ensures that all online transactions are properly documented and linked to corresponding sales records for accurate financial tracking and reconciliation.
Limitations

The study has the following limitations:
Maintenance and System Updates
The system requires regular maintenance and updates to ensure continued security, data integrity, and optimal performance. These updates may include system enhancements, bug fixes, and database optimization. However, the maintenance and administration of the system will be the responsibility of the system administrator or designated authorized personnel of Boss Rap Motor Shop after deployment. The proponents will no longer handle post-deployment maintenance once the system is implemented and turned over to the client.
Internet Dependency (Online System Requirement)
The system is web-based and requires a stable internet connection to function. In cases of internet disruption or power outages affecting connectivity, real-time system access and transaction recording will be temporarily unavailable. During such instances, transactions may be recorded manually and encoded into the system once connectivity is restored.

Initial Manual Data Encoding for Product and Customer Information
The system requires initial manual encoding of product details and customer information during setup and registration. While barcode scanning is used during transactions, product data must still be manually encoded into the system beforehand.
Limited Automation in Membership Perks System
Although the system includes a QR code-based membership module, the loyalty or reward system is limited to basic tracking of customer purchases and does not include advanced automated reward computation or complex tier-based membership levels.
For example, the system does not support advanced reward mechanisms such as progressive point multipliers (e.g., increased points for VIP or high-value customers), dynamic discount tiers based on cumulative or monthly spending thresholds, or automated promotional rules such as “buy X get Y” schemes triggered by customer purchase history or behavioral analysis.
Dependence on User Input Accuracy
The accuracy of inventory, sales, and customer records depends on correct user input. Any incorrect encoding of data may affect reports, inventory counts, and transaction records.

METHODOLOGY

Research Design

This study employs a descriptive developmental research design. The descriptive method is used to examine and understand the existing processes of Boss Rap Motor Shop in managing inventory, sales transactions, and customer information. It focuses on analyzing the current manual and semi-digital practices, particularly in recording product stocks, processing sales, and handling customer data.

On the other hand, the developmental method is used in the design and development of a Web-Based Inventory, Sales, and Membership Management System for Boss Rap Motor Shop. This method focuses on creating a functional system that addresses the identified issues in the current operational setup and improves business processes through system automation and integration.

The purpose of this research design is to assess the current workflow of the shop and serve as a basis for developing a system that improves efficiency, accuracy, and reliability in business operations. Data were gathered through interviews, observation, and document review to identify issues such as manual encoding errors, fragmented records, and difficulty in tracking transactions and customer history.

Overall, this research design ensures that the proposed system is grounded on actual operational needs and is capable of improving the overall management of inventory, sales, and customer information at Boss Rap Motor Shop.

Research Locale

The study was conducted at Boss Rap Motor Shop, a local business that sells motorcycle parts and accessories. The shop currently operates using manual recording methods for inventory and sales transactions, supplemented by informal digital communication such as Facebook inquiries for customer orders.
At present, product stocks and sales are recorded through handwritten logs, which limits real-time monitoring and increases the possibility of errors and inconsistencies. Customer information from both in-store and online transactions is also not systematically stored, making it difficult to track purchase history and repeat customers.
These limitations highlight the need for a more organized and centralized system for managing business operations. In response, the proposed Web-Based Inventory, Sales, and Membership Management System aim to improve data handling by providing a centralized platform for inventory, sales, and customer membership records, thereby enhancing operational efficiency and accuracy at Boss Rap Motor Shop.

Respondents of the Study

The respondents of the study were the shop owner and selected employees of Boss Rap Motor Shop. These individuals were chosen because they are directly involved in the daily operations of the business, particularly in inventory management, sales processing, and customer handling.
The study used purposive sampling, selecting respondents who have direct knowledge and experience in the shop’s operational processes. Their participation provided essential information on how inventory is recorded, how sales transactions are processed (including walk-in and Facebook-based orders), and how customer data is currently managed.
Their responses were crucial in identifying existing challenges in the current system, such as difficulties in monitoring stock levels, delays in recording transactions, and the lack of a centralized customer management system. These findings served as the basis for developing the proposed Web-Based Inventory, Sales, and Membership Management System for Boss Rap Motor Shop.

Data Gathering Procedure

The researchers employed several methods to collect the necessary data for the study. These methods were used to gain a comprehensive understanding of the current operational processes of Boss Rap Motor Shop, particularly in terms of inventory management, sales transactions, and customer information handling.


Interview
The researchers conducted interviews with the shop owner and selected employees of Boss Rap Motor Shop. The interview aimed to gather detailed information regarding the current processes used in managing inventory, recording sales transactions (including walk-in and Facebook-based orders), and handling customer information. It also helped identify the challenges and problems encountered in the existing manual system, such as difficulties in tracking stock levels, delays in transaction recording, and lack of centralized customer data.
Observation
The researchers observed the actual day-to-day operations of the shop, focusing on how inventory is recorded, how sales transactions are processed, and how customer inquiries are handled. This method allowed the researchers to directly understand the workflow of the shop, including manual encoding practices and the handling of both in-store and online transactions. Observation provided firsthand insight into inefficiencies in the current system that are not always fully expressed during interviews.
Document Review
The researchers reviewed existing documents such as product lists, handwritten inventory records, and sales logs used by the shop. These documents were analyzed to understand how data is currently stored, organized, and updated. The review helped identify inconsistencies, redundancy, and limitations in the manual recording system, which supported the justification for developing a centralized web-based system.
System Development Method
The researchers used the Waterfall Model in developing the proposed system. The Waterfall Model is a linear and sequential software development approach in which each phase must be completed before proceeding to the next. This model is suitable for the study because it follows a structured process in developing a Web-Based Inventory, Sales, and Membership Management System for Boss Rap Motor Shop.
Requirement Analysis
In this phase, the researchers gathered information about the current system used by Boss Rap Motor Shop. Data were collected through interviews, observation, and document review to identify the problems encountered in the manual and semi-digital processes. The researchers analyzed issues related to inventory management, sales recording (including Facebook-based transactions), and customer information handling. The system requirements were then determined based on these findings, including the need for inventory automation, barcode-based product identification, QR code-based membership, and reporting features.
System Design
In this phase, the researchers designed the overall structure of the system. This includes the design of the database structure, user interface, and system modules such as inventory management, sales management, membership management, reporting, and online payment recording. The design also defined how barcode scanning will be used for product identification and how QR codes will be implemented for customer membership cards.
System Development
In this phase, the actual development of the web-based system was carried out. The researchers used appropriate programming languages and database tools to build the system according to the approved design. The modules for inventory, sales, membership, reporting, and payment recording were developed and integrated into a centralized platform.
System Testing
After development, the system was tested to ensure that all functionalities work correctly. This includes testing of inventory updates, sales transactions, barcode scanning for product identification, QR code scanning for membership verification, and report generation. Errors and issues identified during testing were corrected to improve system performance, accuracy, and reliability.
System Implementation
After successful testing, the system was implemented and deployed for evaluation at Boss Rap Motor Shop. The system was presented to the shop owner and end users for initial use and assessment. Feedback from the implementation stage was considered for further improvement and refinement of the system.

Database Design

The database was designed to store and manage all essential information required in the operation of Boss Rap Motor Shop. It ensures that all records are centralized, secure, and updated in real time.
The system stores the following types of information:
Product Information – includes product name, description, category, price, and stock quantity 
Sales Transactions – includes transaction date, purchased items, quantities, total amount, and payment details 
Customer Membership Information – includes customer name, contact number, address, membership ID, and QR code 
User Accounts – includes system users such as the shop owner and employees, along with their login credentials and roles 
This centralized database allows accurate monitoring of business operations and ensures proper tracking of inventory, sales, and customer records.

System Modules

The system is composed of several functional modules, each designed to handle specific business processes of Boss Rap Motor Shop. These modules work together to ensure efficient management of inventory, sales transactions, customer membership, and reporting.


Inventory Management Module
The Inventory Management Module is responsible for managing and monitoring all product-related information in the system. It allows users to add new products, update existing product details such as name, category, and price, and monitor stock levels. All product records are stored in a centralized database, where each item is assigned a unique identifier for easy tracking.

In operation, the user inputs product information into the system, including product name, category, price, and quantity. Once saved, the system stores the data and makes it available for use in other modules, particularly during sales transactions. When a product is sold or manually updated, the system automatically adjusts the stock level in real time. This ensures that inventory records remain accurate and up to date. Through this module, the system minimizes errors associated with manual tracking and improves the efficiency of inventory monitoring.

Sales Management Module

The Sales Management Module handles the recording and processing of all sales transactions within the system. It allows users to select products, compute total amounts, record transactions, and automatically update inventory. The module supports both walk-in and online transactions, ensuring that all sales are recorded in a centralized system. Additionally, the Sales Management Module supports multiple payment methods to accommodate different customer preferences, including cash payments and digital payments such as e-wallets or online banking. This allows the system to handle both traditional and modern transaction types within a single platform.

During a transaction, the user selects products either by scanning their barcodes or by manually searching the product list. Once selected, the system retrieves product details such as name, price, and available stock, and adds them to the transaction list. The system then automatically calculates the total amount. After confirming the transaction, the system records the sale and deducts the corresponding quantity from the inventory. This process ensures accurate and efficient transaction handling while reducing human error and improving overall speed.


Barcode-Based Product Identification

To improve efficiency and accuracy in product selection, the system integrates barcode scanning technology. Each product is assigned a unique barcode that is linked to its record in the database. When a barcode is scanned during a transaction, the system automatically retrieves the corresponding product information and adds it to the transaction list.

This eliminates the need for manual data entry, reducing processing time and minimizing errors. The system also includes a manual search option as a fallback in case the barcode cannot be scanned. The use of barcode technology enhances transaction speed, ensures accurate product identification, and supports real-time inventory updates.

Membership Management Module

The Membership Management Module is designed to register and manage customer information while supporting customer retention strategies. It enables the system to store customer details, track purchase history, and identify repeat or high-value customers.

To become a member, customers are required to provide basic information such as full name, contact number, address, and valid identification. Once registered, the system creates a unique membership record for each customer and stores it in a centralized database. This allows easy retrieval and management of customer information during transactions.

During system use, customer membership can be accessed to link transactions to specific individuals, allowing the system to maintain a record of purchase history. This supports better customer service and enables the business to analyze buying behavior for marketing and retention purposes.



QR Code-Based Membership Identification

The system incorporates QR code technology to improve customer identification and transaction efficiency. Upon registration, each member is assigned a unique QR code that can be printed or stored digitally.
During transactions, the QR code is scanned to instantly retrieve customer information from the system. This process allows purchases to be automatically linked to the customer’s account and updates their transaction history in real time. As a result, the system reduces manual data retrieval, minimizes processing time, and improves accuracy in identifying customers.

This feature also supports the tracking of customer purchasing behavior, enabling the shop to identify repeat and high-value customers. 

Reporting Module

The Reporting Module is responsible for generating reports that support business monitoring and decision-making. It provides users with access to important data such as sales performance, inventory status, and customer activity.

The system collects data from various modules and processes it to generate organized reports. Users can request reports based on specific criteria, such as date range or type of transaction. Once generated, the reports can be viewed on the system or printed for documentation purposes. This module eliminates the need for manual report preparation and ensures that business data is accurate, consistent, and readily available.

Through this module, the shop owner can effectively monitor daily operations, evaluate performance, and make informed decisions based on real-time data.

The designed features from the previous phase were translated into functional modules, including inventory management, sales management, membership management, reporting, and online payment recording.

The researchers developed the user interface components such as the login page, dashboard, and module interfaces for inventory, sales, and membership. System Architecture
 
The proposed Web-Based Inventory, Sales, and Membership Card System for Boss Rap Motor Shop utilize a three-tier web architecture. This structure organizes system components into separate layers, enabling efficient processing, scalability, and maintainability. The system is composed of three main layers: the presentation layer, the application layer, and the database layer.

The presentation layer serves as the user interface of the system. It consists of web-based pages accessed through a web browser. The shop owner and employees can log in to the system using internet-connected devices such as computers or laptops. This layer allows users to manage products, record sales transactions, register customer membership information, and access system reports and records.

The application layer handles the processing of user requests and contains the core business logic of the system. It processes all inputs submitted through the presentation layer, such as product entries and sales transactions. It is also responsible for executing system functions including inventory updates, sales computation, and data validation before storing or retrieving information from the database.

The database layer is responsible for the secure storage and management of all system data. This includes product inventories, sales records, membership information, and user accounts. A database management system is used to ensure data integrity, security, and efficient retrieval and updating of records when required.

Overall, the three-tier architecture allows the system to separate user interface, processing logic, and data storage. This improves system performance, enhances security, and ensures better organization of data. It also enables efficient management of inventory, sales, and membership records within the system.
